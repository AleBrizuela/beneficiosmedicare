PLAN TYPES

Understand Your Medicare Options

There are several kinds of Medicare plan, each with different coverage, costs and benefits. We help you work out which one suits you.

🏥

Medicare Advantage

All-in-one coverage combining Part A, Part B and usually Part D. Includes prescriptions and extra benefits.

📋

Medigap Plans

Supplemental insurance covering the costs Original Medicare does not pay. Choose from 10 standardized plans.

💊

Part D Prescriptions

Prescription drug coverage. Available as a standalone plan or built into Medicare Advantage.

👥

Special Needs Plans (SNP)

Coverage tailored to people with specific conditions or circumstances. Available in certain regions.
